Three steps, about 15 minutes, on your phone.

1

Pick your role and language

Collections, field sales, credit analyst or data analyst. English or Hindi.

2

Talk to the interviewer

Answer out loud. The interviewer asks follow-ups and plays the customer in role-play rounds.

3

Get your score and fixes

A score for each round, what you did well, the 3 things to fix, and where you rank against others for the same role.

Questions

Is the demo a real AI interviewer?

The demo is a clickable prototype. It uses your browser's voice input where available and scores your answers against what interviewers look for, to show how the product will work. The launch version uses a full voice AI interviewer.

Do I need to speak, or can I type?

Both work. Speaking is closer to a real interview, so we recommend it once you're comfortable.

Which languages?

English and Hindi at launch for field roles. More regional languages come next, starting with the ones our users ask for most.

Will HireMate get me a job?

No product can promise that, and you should be wary of anyone who does. HireMate helps you walk into the interview prepared and knowing what to fix.
