HireMate: practise the real interview, in your language

An AI mock-interview platform for Indian freshers and early-career switchers going into BFSI and analyst roles. It covers pricing, the path to 10,000 users, the MVP and the metrics, with a live landing page and a clickable prototype.

1Executive summary

Entry-level hiring in India is growing, but most candidates walk in unprepared and nobody tells them why they were rejected. HireMate runs the real interview for four high-volume roles: Data Analyst, Credit Analyst, Field Sales / Relationship Executive and Collections Officer. It does this in English or Hindi, then scores the answer against others going for the same role and lists what to fix.

Candidates pay small amounts, starting with a free first mock and ₹99 per mock after that. Once the candidate pool is big enough, the larger revenue comes from employers, who pay a success fee only when they hire a pre-screened candidate. The plan reaches 10,000 users in 6 months through colleges, WhatsApp job groups and a shareable score card.

Our survey says the real competitor is ChatGPT, not Apna. That's what respondents already use to prepare. So the pitch isn't "AI mock interviews". It's the things ChatGPT doesn't do: a voice role-play that pushes back, a score against other candidates, and questions taken from how NBFCs actually interview.

3Pricing strategy

Model: freemium plus small one-time payments, with a time-boxed pass instead of a monthly subscription. Job searching happens in bursts: people prepare hard for a few weeks and then stop. A pass that ends after 90 days fits that pattern, and it never auto-renews on someone who has already got a job.

Changed after the survey Assumed
When asked how they'd prefer to pay, 8 of 14 respondents chose "monthly unlimited" and none chose the 10-mock pack. So the ₹599 tier is now framed as unlimited for 90 days instead of "30 mocks". It's the same cost ceiling for us, but it answers what people actually asked for. The ₹499 pack stays for now as a price anchor. If it still sells under 5% of paid orders by month 2, it goes.

Feedback I'd value, Sir
