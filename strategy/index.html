HireMate: practise the real interview, in your language

An AI mock-interview platform for Indian freshers and early-career switchers going into BFSI and analyst roles. It covers pricing, the path to 10,000 users, the MVP and the metrics, with a live landing page and a clickable prototype.

1Executive summary

Entry-level hiring in India is growing, but most candidates walk in unprepared and nobody tells them why they were rejected. HireMate runs the real interview for four high-volume roles: Data Analyst, Credit Analyst, Field Sales / Relationship Executive and Collections Officer. It does this in English or Hindi, then scores the answer against others going for the same role and lists what to fix.

Candidates pay small amounts, starting with a free first mock and ₹99 per mock after that. Colleges and training institutes buy seats for their students. Once the pool is big enough, NBFCs pay a success fee, but only after a hire has stayed 30 days. The plan reaches 10,000 users in 6 months through colleges, WhatsApp job groups and a shareable score card.

Our survey says the real competitor is ChatGPT, not Apna. That's what respondents already use to prepare. So the pitch isn't "AI mock interviews". 3Pricing strategy

Model: freemium plus small one-time payments, a time-boxed pass instead of a monthly subscription, and seats sold to colleges and institutes. Job searching happens in bursts: people prepare hard for a few weeks and then stop. A pass that ends after 90 days fits that pattern, and it never auto-renews on someone who has already got a job.

Changed after the survey Assumed
When asked how they'd prefer to pay, 8 of 14 survey respondents chose "monthly unlimited" and none chose the 10-mock pack. So the ₹599 tier became unlimited for 90 days, and the ₹499 pack was dropped: no survey respondent and no persona chose it.
Open conflict: all 5 synthetic candidate personas rejected monthly plans and preferred ₹99–₹200 once, before a specific interview. The real survey was small and mostly my own network. Launch with both the ₹99 single and the ₹599 pass, and let real purchases decide.

5MVP strategy and problem validation

What we did

Market research from four AI tools, cross-checked, with every number used here verified at the original source (6 errors caught and removed). This was followed by a self-hosted survey with a screener (19–30 Sep), then 14 synthetic persona interviews: 7 personas (5 candidates, 2 hiring managers), each run separately in ChatGPT and Gemini, answering the same interview guide. Real interviews didn't happen in time; that is the main gap, and the next step.

7 AI errors caught and fixed in this round, on top of the 6 in the market research. Examples: Gemini called a weekend RTGS credit a sign of forgery (RTGS has run 24×7 since Dec 2020), and 4 of its "model answers" would break fair-practice rules (fake urgency, compulsory insurance, threat-like escalation, social-media tracing). An interview coach that teaches rule-breaking answers is a liability, so every scenario is checked before it ships.

What that changes: the lead message is role-play depth and honest feedback, the gap ChatGPT leaves ("it always agrees with me"). Hindi and Kannada stay for field roles. Candidates are a weak payer on their own, so colleges, institutes and NBFCs carry the revenue.

How far to trust the persona interviews: they are AI-simulated, not real users. Lines that praise the idea are ignored, because they echo the prompt. The objections (no monthly plans, "don't shout at me more", "a score means nothing", data privacy) are kept, because the AIs produced them against the grain. Gemini invented 4 numbers despite instructions, and none are used. All 14 transcripts and the scoring rules are kept in the research folder.

7Landing page, prototype and promo posts

Clickable prototype

Pick a role and a language, then answer out loud (speech-to-text) or type. After every answer the interviewer pushes back, e.g. a job-loss customer who then threatens to complain to RBI, or a DSA offering files for a cash cut. You get a score, a red-line check against RBI recovery rules and fair selling, a ranking band, strengths, fixes and a 3-step plan. Collections and Sales work in Hindi too. Scoring is keyword-based, as a stand-in for the LLM. Try a wrong answer on purpose, like "OK sir, I'll call at 7:30".
