# HireMate: User Interview Script (for recording)

Read this out as it is. Takes 20–25 minutes. Questions in **bold** are the must-asks if time is short.

---

## Before you press record

- Quiet place, phone on charge, second device recording as backup
- Have the note template open: write down **surprises** as you hear them
- Don't mention HireMate or AI until the very end

---

## PART 1: Job seekers (interviews 1–4)

### Opening + recording consent (1 min)

> "Hi [Name], thank you for taking the time. I'm studying how freshers and early-career people prepare for job interviews. It's for my Product Management program with IIT Roorkee and Masai.
>
> There are no right or wrong answers. I only want to learn from your experience, and I'm not selling anything.
>
> **Is it okay if I record this call?** The recording is only for my notes. I won't share it, and I won't use your name anywhere."

*(Wait for a clear "yes". Once recording, ask again: "Just confirming on record, you're okay with this being recorded?")*

### A. About you (3 min)

1. **Tell me a bit about yourself: what are you doing right now?**
2. What kind of jobs are you applying for?
3. How long have you been looking?
4. In interviews, which language do you usually speak? And at home or with friends?

### B. The last interview: the story (8–10 min) ⭐

5. **Tell me about the last interview you went for. Start from how you got the call.**
6. How many rounds were there? What happened in each one?
7. **Which question or moment was the hardest? What did you say?**
8. How did it end? Did you hear back from them?
9. *(If not selected)* **Did anyone tell you why you weren't selected?**
10. What do *you* think went wrong?

**Follow-ups (use their exact words, and never ask "why"):**
- "You said ___. Tell me more about that."
- "What happened after that?"
- "Can you give me an example?"
- "How did you feel at that moment?"

### C. How they prepared (5 min)

11. **What did you do to prepare for that interview?**
12. How did you know what questions they might ask?
13. Have you ever practised with someone before an interview? Walk me through it.
14. **Have you used any app or website to practise interviews? What happened? Do you still use it?**

### D. Money and trust (3 min)

15. **Have you paid for anything in your job search: resume, Naukri, a course, coaching?** What did you get? Was it worth it?
16. Has anyone ever asked you for money to get you a job? What happened?

### E. Closing (2 min)

17. **If you had a magic wand, what one thing would you change about how you prepare for interviews?**
18. Is there anything I didn't ask that I should have?
19. Do you know one or two people in a similar situation who might talk to me?

> "That's everything. Thank you so much, this really helps. If you'd like, I'll share what I learn once I've spoken to everyone."

*(Stop the recording. Write down the 3 things that surprised you most, straight away.)*

## PART 2: Hiring manager (interview 5)

### Opening + recording consent (1 min)

> "Hi [Name], thank you for your time. I'm studying how companies hire freshers and early-career people for BFSI roles, for my PM program with IIT Roorkee and Masai.
>
> I won't ask for any company numbers or confidential information, only your experience of interviewing people.
>
> **Is it okay if I record this call?** It's only for my notes. I won't share it or use your name or your company's name."

### A. Their hiring (4 min)

1. **What roles do you hire for, and how often do you interview freshers or people with 1–3 years' experience?**
2. Walk me through how a typical interview for a field / credit role runs.

### B. The last hiring round: the story (8 min) ⭐

3. **Tell me about the last time you hired for one of these roles. How did you decide who to take?**
4. **For the people you didn't select, what made them fall short? Can you give me an example of an answer that didn't work?**
5. What does a candidate who is "ready" look like to you?
6. Do you tell candidates why they weren't selected? What stops you?

### C. After hiring (5 min)

7. Of the people you hired, how did the first two or three months go?
8. **When someone leaves early, what usually happened?**
9. What do you do today to find better candidates? What has worked, and what hasn't?

### D. Closing (2 min)

10. **If you could know one thing about every candidate before the interview, what would it be?**
11. Is there anything I should have asked?

> "Thank you. This helps me a lot to understand the hiring side."

## Do and don't (keep this visible during the call)

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask about the **last** time something happened | Ask "Would you use…?" or "Would you pay…?" |
| Repeat their words back: "You said ___…" | Say "why"; use "tell me more" instead |
| Stay quiet after they answer; they often add the best part | Explain HireMate or pitch anything |
| Write down surprises and contradictions first | Correct them or give interview advice mid-call |
| Let them go off-topic for a bit | Rush to the next question |

## After each interview (10 min)

1. Fill in the note template (in `user-research-plan.md`, section 6)
2. Copy 2–3 exact quotes worth using
3. Mark hypotheses H1–H7: ✔ / ✘ / ?
4. Save the recording as `interview-01-[role].m4a` (no names in file names)
5. Delete recordings once the notes are done and the project is submitted
