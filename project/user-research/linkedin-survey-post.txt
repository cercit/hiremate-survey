How did you prepare for your first job interview?

For me it was one night before, a few YouTube videos and my friend asking me "tell me about yourself" three times. That was my full preparation. Or something like that.

I'm doing the Product Management program with IIT Roorkee and Masai, and we're building HireMate, an interview practice product for freshers and early-career people. Before building anything I want to hear from real job seekers, not guess.

One number stayed with me while researching. Apna's Interview Readiness Index (2025) says only 21% of freshers feel ready going into an interview. I want to understand the other 79%.

The survey is anonymous and takes 5 minutes: https://forms.gle/XBf7VDJ5VEfcx2Et9

It's for you if:
- you are a student or fresher looking for your first job, or
- you have less than 3 years of experience and are thinking of a switch

I'm especially keen to hear from people going for Data Analyst, Credit Analyst, Field Sales / Relationship Executive or Collections roles in banks and NBFCs.

If you are past this stage, a repost or sharing it in your college or alumni group would help me a lot. I will share what I learn from the responses in a follow-up post.
