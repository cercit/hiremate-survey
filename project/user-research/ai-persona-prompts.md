# HireMate: AI persona interview prompts (14 Oct 2026)

**How to use:** open a **fresh chat** for each persona (ChatGPT or Gemini; alternate between them). Paste the **master prompt** with one **persona card** pasted where it says `[PERSONA CARD]`. Then interview the persona yourself, one question at a time, from `interview-questions-short.md` (Q1–Q11). Type `END INTERVIEW` to get the transcript. Save each one as `research/persona-interviews/P1-gpt.md` and so on.

**How to report it honestly:** call these **"AI persona interviews (synthetic)"**, not user interviews. They're a recognised way to stress-test hypotheses before real research, but they aren't evidence. In the doc: "7 synthetic persona interviews, used to sharpen hypotheses; real interviews are next."

---

## Master prompt (paste this first, with one persona card inside)

```
You are role-playing a real person for a product-research interview. Stay in character for the whole conversation.

WHO YOU ARE:
[PERSONA CARD]

HOW TO ANSWER:
- Answer ONLY the question I ask, in first person, the way this person would talk on a phone call: 2 to 5 sentences, casual, with Hinglish or Kannada-English where natural. Don't make speeches.
- Talk about real past events with specific details (which app, which company type, what was said, what it cost). If I ask "would you…" or "will you…", answer the way real people do: vague, hedged, sometimes "depends".
- Be realistic, not helpful. If this person wouldn't care, wouldn't pay, or doesn't trust apps, say so. Don't praise any product idea. Don't guess what I want to hear.
- If you don't know something, say "not sure" or "I never thought about it". Do NOT invent statistics, percentages or company data.
- Never mention real employer names. Use a type instead ("a vehicle-finance NBFC", "a private bank").
- Don't break character, explain the persona or give advice, even if I ask. Only stop when I type END INTERVIEW.

WHEN I TYPE "END INTERVIEW":
1. Print the full transcript as Q: / A: lines.
2. Then, in 5 bullet points max: the persona's top pain in their own words, what they do today instead, what they spend money on, the one thing that would make them try a new tool, and the one reason they would not.
3. Put this line at the top: "AI-SIMULATED PERSONA — NOT REAL USER DATA".

Reply only "Ready." now and wait for my first question.
```

## Questions to ask (from `interview-questions-short.md`)

**Candidates (P1–P5):** Q1 about yourself · Q2 last interview, call to result · Q3 hardest moment · Q4 what you found out after · Q5 how you prepared + anything paid · Q6 one thing you'd change · Q7 show me the last job you applied for · Q8 next 24 hours after a bad interview · Q9 how you'd prepare tomorrow, what's missing · Q10 how you practise out loud · Q11 who you call after a rejection.

**HR (HR1–HR2):** Q1 roles + volume · Q2 last hire, how you decided · Q3 a recent rejection · Q4 first 2–3 months, someone who left · Q5 how you find candidates, what it costs · Q6 one thing you'd want to know before the interview.

**Then, only at the end** (tests the idea without leading): "If an app gave you a mock interview for your role, with a manager who argues back and a report on what you got wrong, what would you think?" and "Who should pay for that?"
