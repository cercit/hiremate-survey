# HireMate: Short interview set (record → transcribe → analyse)

7 interviews: **5 job seekers + 2 HR / hiring managers**. 6 open-ended questions each, 15–20 min.

---

## Opening (both groups)

> "Thank you for your time. This is for my Product Management program with IIT Roorkee and Masai. There are no right or wrong answers, I just want to learn from your experience. Is it okay if I record this? It's only for my notes, and I won't use your name."

*(Once recording starts, ask again so the "yes" is on the recording.)*

---

## A. Job seekers (5 interviews)

| # | Question | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about yourself: what you're doing now and what kind of job you're looking for. | Segment, role, stage |
| 2 | **Walk me through the last interview you attended, from the call until the result.** | The real story (H1, H3) |
| 3 | What was the hardest moment in that interview, and what did you say? | Failure point by role (H3, H4) |
| 4 | After the result, what did you find out about why it went that way? | Feedback gap (H2) |
| 5 | Tell me how you prepared for it, and anything you've paid for in your job search. | Current solutions + spend (H1, H5, H6) |
| 6 | If you could change one thing about how you prepare for interviews, what would it be? | Unmet need, their words |

**Follow-ups (use their words):** "Tell me more about that." · "What happened next?" · "Can you give me an example?"

**Add if time allows (added 27 Sep; ask before you describe HireMate):**
- 7. "Show me the last job you applied for: where you found it, what you sent, what happened next."
- 8. "The last time an interview went badly, what did you do in the next 24 hours?"
- 9. "If you had to prepare for your next interview tomorrow, how would you do it? What's missing from that?"
- If they're a switcher: "Which jobs do you think you can realistically get with your experience?" (tests the role-fit idea)
- 10. "How do you practise out loud: who's with you, and what do you actually say?"
- 11. "If you're rejected after a final round, who do you call first, and what do you tell them the reason was?"

---

## B. HR / hiring managers (2 interviews)

*Pick people from outside your own company. Don't ask for company numbers.*

| # | Question | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tell me about the roles you hire for, and how many freshers or early-career people you interview. | Context, volume |
| 2 | **Walk me through the last time you hired for one of these roles. How did you decide?** | The real hiring process |
| 3 | Think of a candidate you rejected recently. What made them fall short? | Candidate gaps → MVP scenarios |
| 4 | What happens with new hires in their first 2–3 months? Tell me about someone who left early. | Attrition pain (H7) |
| 5 | How do you find candidates today, and what does it cost you in time or money? | Current solutions, willingness to pay (H7) |
| 6 | If you could know one thing about every candidate before the interview, what would it be? | What the employer product must show |

---

## Closing (both)

> "Is there anything I should have asked but didn't?"
> "Do you know someone else I could talk to?"

---

## Record → transcribe → analyse

1. **Record:** phone call recorder, Google Meet recording, or a voice recorder app, on 2 devices if you can.
2. **Transcribe** (free options):
   - Google Recorder app (Pixel) or Samsung Voice Recorder: automatic transcript
   - Google Meet: turn on transcripts (Workspace accounts)
   - Upload the audio to **Gemini** or **ChatGPT**: "Transcribe this interview word for word. Label speakers as Interviewer and Participant. Keep Hindi as spoken, and add an English translation in brackets."
3. **Clean it up:** replace names with `P1…P5`, `HR1`, `HR2`. Remove company names and phone numbers.
4. **Save** as `user-research/transcripts/P1-field-sales.txt`, `HR1.txt`, and so on.
5. **Send them to me:** I'll pull out quotes, themes and the H1–H7 scorecard, and write the MVP problem-validation section from them.
