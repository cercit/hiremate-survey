You are a labour-market research analyst focused on India. I am building a launch
strategy for an AI mock-interview platform targeting (a) fresh graduates entering
the workforce and (b) employees with under 3 years' experience switching their
first job. Target roles: Data Analyst, Credit Analyst, Field Sales / Relationship
Executive (banks and NBFCs), Collections Officer.

Research and answer, with a source + year for every number:

1. Entry-level hiring share: what % of job openings in India are for 0-3 years'
   experience? Trend 2023-2026. (Check Naukri JobSpeak, TeamLease Employment
   Outlook, India Skills Report, foundit/Indeed reports.)
2. Pool size: annual graduates in India (engineering, commerce, BBA/MBA) and
   estimated number of 0-3 yr employees actively job switching.
3. BFSI hiring volume: annual hiring numbers for field sales, collections and
   credit roles at Indian banks and NBFCs; attrition rates in these roles.
4. Salary bands (monthly, INR) for each of the 4 target roles at entry level and
   at 1-3 years.
5. Interview process for each role: typical rounds, language (English vs Hindi/
   regional), what gets candidates rejected.
6. Willingness to pay: what do Indian job seekers currently spend on interview
   prep, resume services and job assistance? Any survey data.
7. Competitors in India and their current pricing: InterviewBuddy, Pramp,
   Exponent, Final Round AI, Unstop, Apna, WorkIndia, Naukri paid services, any
   AI interview tools. Note which cover BFSI roles and regional languages.
8. Employer side: what do Indian NBFCs/banks pay recruitment agencies per hire
   for field roles (fixed fee or % of CTC)?
9. Regulatory/trust: any rules or common scams around charging job seekers
   placement fees in India.

Output format: one table per question (Metric | Value | Source | Year). Mark
anything estimated or unverified as "ESTIMATE". End with 5 key insights for a
launch strategy. Do not invent numbers; say "not found" instead.
