**5 key insights for launch strategy**

1. **Large, underserved entry-level pool with rising non-IT demand**: 0-3 year hiring is growing (especially non-IT BFSI-adjacent sectors), but candidates face high competition (e.g., 9.5:1 in engineering). Focus on Data Analyst + BFSI frontline (sales/collections/credit) where volume is high and soft-skills/language gaps are acute.

2. **High attrition + volume in field BFSI roles creates recurring need**: Private bank/NBFC field sales and collections see 25–45% attrition and heavy entry-level hiring. Mock interviews targeting sales pitch, recovery role-play, and bilingual (Hindi/regional + English) practice address real rejection points.

3. **Low preparedness + willingness for low-cost AI tools**: Only ~32% feel interview-ready; users of mocks/AI report higher confidence. Price in the ₹500–2,500 range (session or short subscription) to undercut human services (InterviewBuddy) while being far cheaper than USD tools. Resume + AI mock bundle aligns with 87% AI-resume usage.

4. **Differentiation opportunity**: Most competitors are English/tech-focused or pure job boards. Prioritize BFSI-specific scenarios (credit cases, field sales role-plays, collections conversations) + Hindi/regional language support + India company data. Employer-side angle (agency fees 8–15% of CTC) could support B2B freemium or co-branded tools for NBFCs reducing agency dependency.

5. **Trust & regulatory positioning is critical**: Explicitly free of placement fees, transparent pricing, and education against scams builds credibility. Partner with legitimate platforms (Apna, Naukri, Unstop) or colleges for distribution; avoid any fee structure that could be confused with illegal placement charges.
