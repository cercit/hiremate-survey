## Top 5 takeaways for the launch document

1. **The market fits your thesis.** 98% of BFSI postings accept ≤3 yrs experience, and fresher hiring grows 15% a year. But freshers face 9.5 candidates per job in engineering, and only 21% feel interview-ready.
2. **Attrition keeps customers coming back.** Frontline BFSI turnover is 25–77%, so the same user job-hunts again within 1–2 years.
3. **Apna is the real competitor.** It already offers free AI mock interviews in Hindi for entry-level jobs. HireMate has to be **deeper, not just in the same language**: role-specific BFSI scenarios (a collections call role-play, a credit case), a benchmark score and a growth plan.
4. **Micro-pricing fits.** ₹99 is 0.5% of a field officer's monthly pay. Global tools cost ₹1,000+ a month and cover only English tech roles.
5. **Candidates pay small, employers pay big.** Charging candidates a placement fee looks like a scam and may fall under the draft bill. An employer success fee is the industry norm and fits NBFCs' hiring pain.
