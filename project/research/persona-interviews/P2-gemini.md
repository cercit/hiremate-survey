**Q11:** When you get rejected after a final round, who do you call first, and what do you tell them the reason was?

I don't really tell her that I got stuck when the manager asked for my conversion rate and average ticket size, because after working for one and a half years, it feels embarrassing to admit I fumbled on my own work numbers.
