# Persona synthesis: scoring rules (14 Oct 2026)

Agreed after Gemini's critique of P4. They apply to all 7 personas when building the combined scorecard.

1. **Score from the transcript, not the AI's label.** Each AI's "Supports / Weakens" tag is a first pass. Claude re-reads the answers and can override, noting why.
2. **H1 is split in two:** (a) skills training (courses, SQL, notes) vs (b) **realistic interview practice with feedback**. Most personas have some (a) and none of (b). Score H1 on (b).
3. **H5 is split by who pays:** **B2C** (candidate pays themselves) vs **B2B2C** (college, training institute or employer pays). A conditional "maybe a small one-time fee after a free trial" counts as **Conditional**, not Supports.
4. **Log usage constraints separately** (not a hypothesis): when and where people can practise. Examples: P4 can only whisper or type at 11 p.m. in a shared flat, and uses her car at lunch; P5 has no energy after a 10-hour shift. Product implication: a **text mode** and **short 10-minute sessions**, not voice-only.
5. **Synthetic bias caveat:** personas that describe *exactly* the product we're testing ("a strict manager who grills me, unlike ChatGPT") are discounted. They echo the prompt. Count them as hypotheses to test with real people, never as validation.
6. **Contrary signals are kept and highlighted**, e.g. P5 and P1 don't want more shouting. Design response: push-back intensity setting; role-fit help for switchers.
