## 1. Collections Officer / Collection Executive

**Titles:** Collection Officer / Executive, Recovery Officer / Executive, Field Collection Executive, FOS.
**Work:** phone follow-up, field visits, overdue EMI recovery, repayment negotiation, record keeping, escalation to legal recovery.
**Qualification:** broad; many postings accept 10th/12th pass. Mahindra Finance's Prarambh programme hires into Collection Executive roles.
**Salary examples (single postings, not benchmarks):** ₹15–25k, ₹15–18k and ₹18–22k/month (Chennai postings).
**Needs:** local language, two-wheeler and licence, heavy travel.
**Skills:** communication, negotiation, persistence, customer handling, field discipline.
**Regulation:** RBI recovery-agent rules (no intimidation, no third-party intrusion, calls only 8 a.m.–7 p.m., no false representation). IIBF DRA certification is mandatory for recovery agents.
**Rounds [SYNTHESISED]:** HR screen → branch/collection manager → role-play → documentation.
**Rejection triggers [SYNTHESISED]:** aggressive recovery language, can't handle an angry customer, won't travel, weak local language, not aware of recovery rules.

## 6. What AI voice can and can't practise (ChatGPT's view)

**Strong fit:** introductions, communication, empathy, collections negotiation, objection handling, 60-second pitch, target pressure, regulatory responses, business-case reasoning, explaining data to a manager, local-language field talk.
**Needs another workspace:** fake document detection, SQL, Excel, Power BI.
**Needs a human / real world:** field mobility, actual acquisition and collection performance.

**ChatGPT's product view:** start with Collections and Field Sales (most conversational, clear role-plays, local language). Credit comes next. Data Analyst gets stronger once there's a SQL/Excel workspace. Its suggestion: chain scenarios so one conversation tests several skills, e.g. job loss → "I'll complain to RBI" → "manager says call at 7:30 a.m."
