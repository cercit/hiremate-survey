## 5 Key Insights for Launch Strategy

1. **Massive, underserved entry-level pool**: ~8–10M graduates/year, with 0–3 yrs being the fastest-growing hiring segment (+15–18% YoY), but only 28% of engineering openings match this group — huge supply-demand mismatch.  
2. **BFSI churn creates repeat customers**: Field sales and collections roles have 30–50% annual attrition, meaning candidates will re-enter the job market frequently — ideal for subscription or repeat-purchase models.  
3. **Price sensitivity is real but willingness exists**: Indian job seekers already spend ₹500–₹2,000 on resume services and ₹1,500–₹3,000 on mock interviews; an AI platform at ₹500–₹1,500/month could be competitive vs. human-led alternatives.  
4. **Language and role specificity are white spaces**: Most AI tools (Pramp, Exponent, Final Round) are English-only and tech-focused; BFSI roles (sales, collections, credit) require Hindi/regional practice and scenario-based role-plays — a clear differentiation opportunity.  
5. **Trust is critical**: With widespread placement-fee scams, positioning as “employer-paid” or “free for candidates, monetized via B2B” could accelerate adoption; alternatively, transparent, money-back-guaranteed pricing with verifiable outcomes (e.g., interview callbacks) would build credibility.
